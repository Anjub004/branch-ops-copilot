# Returns Policy

Customers may return non-perishable items within 14 days with a receipt. Items must be unused and in original packaging.

Perishable items (fresh produce, dairy, meat, bakery) can only be returned on the same day of purchase, and only if they are spoiled.

Electrical items may be returned within 7 days if faulty. After 7 days the customer is referred to the supplier warranty.

Without a receipt, the supervisor may offer an exchange but never a cash refund.

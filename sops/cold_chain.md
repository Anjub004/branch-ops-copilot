# Cold Chain and Chiller Temperatures

Chillers for dairy and cooked meat must stay between 0 and 5 degrees Celsius.

Freezers must stay at minus 18 degrees Celsius or colder.

Temperatures are recorded on the temperature log three times a day: at opening, at 14:00 and at closing.

If a chiller is above 8 degrees for more than two hours, the stock inside is moved to another chiller and the maintenance team is called. Stock that stayed warm for more than four hours is written off.

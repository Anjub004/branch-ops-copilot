# Store Opening and Closing

The store opens to customers at 08:00 and closes at 21:00 from Monday to Saturday, and 09:00 to 18:00 on Sunday.

The opening supervisor arrives 45 minutes early to disarm the alarm, check chillers and switch on the tills.

At closing, the supervisor checks that all customers have left, the safe is locked and the alarm is armed. The alarm code is never shared over the phone.
